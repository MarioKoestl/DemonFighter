# Asset and Third-Party Licenses

One entry per asset, pack, or borrowed code. Nothing ships without an entry. Template:

```
## <asset or pack name>
- Source: <tool, store, repository, or person>
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- License: <name and link>
- Commercial use: yes / no / with attribution
- Attribution text (if required): ...
- Files: Assets/_Project/Art/... or Assets/_Project/Scripts/...
```

The art validator finds a model's entry by its path or file name, so every model file is named in full below.

## Entries

**Models generated with Meshy.**

All four were generated by Mario with Meshy Text to 3D on the paid Pro plan. On a paid plan the subscriber owns the generated models and may use them commercially without attribution, as long as the models stay private (not published to the Meshy community) and no uploaded source material infringes anyone's copyright. Rights follow the plan at generation time, so cancelling the plan later does not change them. Terms: [Meshy, "What Is the Ownership of the Generated Models"](https://help.meshy.ai/en/articles/10137554-what-is-the-ownership-of-the-generated-models) and [Meshy, "Can I Use Meshy Assets Commercially"](https://help.meshy.ai/en/articles/9992001-can-i-use-meshy-assets-commercially-license-copyright-explained). Keep a PDF of the terms and the Pro invoice in `ArtSource/licenses/`.
